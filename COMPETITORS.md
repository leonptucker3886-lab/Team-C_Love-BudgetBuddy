# BudgetBuddy vs. competitors

## Nearest competitor: Goodbudget
Goodbudget is the closest match: a category ("envelope") budget planner where you set monthly amounts and log spending against them. Other apps compared: YNAB, EveryDollar, Monarch Money and spreadsheet templates.

## What reviewers say about the competition
- **Goodbudget:** praised for simple envelope budgeting; the free tier limits how many envelopes and accounts you get, and the rest is behind a subscription. Manual entry feels dated.
- **YNAB:** powerful and loved by its fans, but the subscription is expensive and the method has a steep learning curve.
- **EveryDollar:** easy zero-based budgeting, but bank sync and the useful features need a premium subscription.
- **Monarch Money:** polished, but subscription-only and needs a bank connection to shine.
- **Spreadsheets:** free and flexible, but no chart or progress bars unless you build them yourself, and clumsy on phones.

## Why BudgetBuddy is better
- **One-time $4.99, everything included.** No subscription, no category limit, no premium tier.
- **No account and no bank login.** Your data stays in your browser; export/import a backup file any time.
- **Instant visuals.** Per-category progress bars that turn red when you overspend, plus a budget-vs-spent chart.
- **Learn it in a minute.** Set income and category budgets, log spending, done. Copy last month's budget forward in one click.
- **Runs anywhere.** A single web page that works in any modern browser on desktop or phone, with dark mode and any currency.

_Competitor details are based on public reviews and app listings; pricing can change._
